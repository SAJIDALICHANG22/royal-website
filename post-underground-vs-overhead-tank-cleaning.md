We clean both underground and overhead tanks, and many customers book both in the same visit. The two types collect dirt differently, so the work is a little different too.

## Underground tanks

Underground tanks often collect the sand and sediment that comes in with the incoming water. Over time this can build up into a thick layer on the floor.

- Larger volume, so draining takes longer
- Sludge and sediment removal is the main job
- Walls, floor and corners are scrubbed, then disinfected and rinsed
- Access through the opening is often tight, so photos of the access point help us plan

## Overhead tanks

Rooftop tanks are exposed to dust, and a poorly fitted cover lets more of it in. Fine sediment and surface buildup are common.

- Plastic, fiberglass and concrete tanks each need a method suited to the surface
- We take care around fittings and connections
- We check the accessible areas around the lid, inlet and outlet for visible issues

## Can I clean both together?

Yes. Booking both in one visit saves time. Mention the number and type of tanks when you ask for a quotation so we can plan the work.
