Most of us only see the water that comes out of the tap. The tank that stores it is out of sight, so it is easy to forget. Here are five signs that it is time to have your tank looked at.

## 1. Visible sediment or sand in the water

If you notice fine sand, silt or small particles in a glass of water, or at the bottom of a bucket, they often come from the tank. Underground tanks in particular collect sediment carried in by incoming water.

## 2. A change in water colour

Water that looks yellowish, brownish or cloudy compared with what you are used to is worth checking. The cause can be the tank, the pipes or the incoming supply, so an inspection helps narrow it down.

## 3. An unusual smell

A musty or stale smell from stored water is another reason to inspect the tank. Tell us what you notice when you call. These details help us understand your concern before the visit.

## 4. Dirt around the lid, inlet or outlet

If the tank cover does not fit properly, dust and dirt can enter. Damaged or loose covers should be fixed or replaced, and the tank cleaned once the cover is sorted.

## 5. You cannot remember the last cleaning

If you do not know when the tank was last cleaned, an inspection is a sensible starting point. The right interval depends on your incoming water, the condition of the tank and how quickly sediment builds up.

> A visibly clean tank alone does not confirm that water is safe to drink. Drinking-water safety also depends on the incoming supply, pipes and treatment.
