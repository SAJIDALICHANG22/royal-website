Most of us only see the water that comes out of the tap. The tank that stores it is usually out of sight, so it is easy to forget. Here is what to look for.

## Signs your tank may need attention

- Sand, silt or small particles in a glass of water
- Water that looks yellowish, brownish or cloudy
- A musty or stale smell from stored water
- Dust or dirt around the tank lid, inlet or outlet
- You cannot remember when the tank was last cleaned

## Why underground and overhead tanks both need care

Underground tanks often collect the sand and sediment carried in by incoming water. Rooftop tanks are exposed to dust, especially when the cover does not fit properly. Both can build up dirt over time without anyone noticing.

## What our cleaning covers

We drain the tank, remove sludge and sediment, scrub and pressure wash the walls, floor and corners, disinfect, and finish with a final rinse and a visual check before handover.

> A visibly clean tank alone does not confirm that water is safe to drink. Drinking-water safety also depends on the incoming supply, pipes and treatment.
