A little preparation makes tank cleaning quicker and keeps the interruption to your water supply short. Here is a simple checklist.

## Before we arrive

1. **Store some water.** The tank being cleaned is taken out of use during the work, so keep enough water in buckets or a spare container for the day.
2. **Clear the access.** Make sure the tank opening and the path to it are free of boxes, cars or other obstacles.
3. **Tell us about restrictions.** For apartments and commercial properties, mention any access rules and the preferred time for the work.
4. **Share your tank details.** Tank type, approximate size and a photo of the opening help us give you a clear quotation and time estimate.

## During the work

We drain the tank, remove sludge and sediment, scrub and pressure wash the walls, floor and corners, disinfect, then rinse. Before handover we do a final rinse and a visual check, and we tell you when the tank is ready for refilling.

## After the cleaning

If we notice an accessible crack, a damaged cover or another concern, we will point it out. Repairs need a separate assessment.
