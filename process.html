How We Clean Your Water Tank

Six clear steps from first check to final rinse.

Check the Tank

We assess its type, condition and access, and discuss the work before starting.

Drain the Remaining Water

The tank is drained so settled dirt and affected surfaces can be reached.

Remove Sludge and Sediment

Accumulated sand, mud and loose debris are collected and removed.

Scrub and Pressure Wash

The walls, floor and corners are cleaned using tools and water pressure suited to the tank's material and condition.

Disinfect

A disinfection stage follows the physical cleaning, using a product intended for water-storage applications according to its instructions.

Rinse and Check

The tank receives a final rinse and a visual check before handover. We explain any observations and advise when it is ready for refilling.

Good to know

Plan for the interruption

The tank being cleaned needs to be taken out of use during the work. Discuss your water requirements when booking so you can prepare for the interruption.

How long the cleaning takes depends on the tank's size, the amount of buildup, drainage and access. We can give you an estimated duration after reviewing the details.
